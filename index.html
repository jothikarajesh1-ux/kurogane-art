Terms & Conditions

1. Public Platform Policy: Every profile, image, video, and comment published on Kurogane is strictly public. There are no private user profiles.

2. Media Upload Formats & Restrictions: Users can upload image files (`.png`, `.jpg`, `.webp`) and video clips (`.mp4`, `.mov`). Video uploads **must not exceed 10 minutes (600 seconds)** in duration.

3. Artwork Adaptation Rights: By uploading reference photos or costume designs, you grant Kurogane non-exclusive permission to use your submissions as drawing references for original anime/chibi artwork.

4. Full User Account Control: Users have full authority to log in, log out, or **delete their account and associated uploaded media at any given time** directly through profile settings.

5. Respect & Content Guidelines: Strictly no explicit, violent, or copyrighted commercial material.
